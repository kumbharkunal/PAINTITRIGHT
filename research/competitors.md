## Overused (avoid)

- **Heroes:** video or carousel heroes with the same eyebrow on every slide; a glossy supercar on black with neon red; water-beading and foam macros.
- **Copy:** "premium", "showroom shine", "turn heads", "perfection is in the detail", "passion meets…", 9H/10H, "No.1", "King of…", "Refuse X" / "X Ends Here".
- **Proof:** marque-logo tickers, inflated counters, press logos, review carousels.
- **Packages:** gimmick tier names, spec tables, "call for price".
- **Process:** the Inspect / Correct / Protect triptych numbered 01/03.
- **Other:** franchise blocks, SEO keyword dumps, floating WhatsApp bubbles, warranty-check portals, "lifetime" with fine print.

## What to beat

- **Detailing Devils' polish:** use their own cars and the paint capability DD doesn't show.
- **CarzSpa's capped "lifetime":** a one-sentence warranty, once the client defines it.
- **XPEL and the Detailing Gang's clarity:** inclusions and timelines (guide prices only if the client agrees).
- **ESR's authority:** real work, not adjectives.
- **Mobile speed:** every benchmark is heavy on video and carousels.

## Positioning

**"One studio paints it, protects it, and answers for the whole job."**
- Film warranties exclude repainted or repaired paint: CarzSpa/Aegis excludes "application on repainted panels"; LLumar says aftermarket or repaired paint isn't covered; XPEL covers the film, not the paint.
- A studio that controls both paint and film can credibly stand behind the combination.
- **Risks:**
  - "Lifetime" must be defined (advertising-claim exposure);
  - "under one roof" isn't unique, so show paint process;
  - never imply OEM certifications they don't hold.

## Delhi context (sourced)

- **Heat:** peaks late May to early June, about 38 °C average with heat waves near 45 °C; dust storms ("andhi"). (Climate of Delhi, Wikipedia)
- **Monsoon:** late June to mid-September, about 797 mm.
- **Road dust:** 56% of PM10 and 38% of PM2.5 (2016 study); construction dust. (Air pollution in Delhi, Wikipedia)
- **Air quality:** Poor to Hazardous, October to February.
- **General guidance (not Delhi-specific):** bird droppings, hard-water spots and tree sap should be removed promptly. Stone chips and tar are a highway brand claim.
- **Unsourced** (don't use): acid fallout and hard-water figures.
