## Address verdict

**Use "130, Hog Market, Rajendra Place, New Delhi 110008".**

- Every source the business controls says Hog Market, Rajendra/Rajindra Place:
  - IG address field
  - reel end cards ("130 HOG MARKET RAJENDRA PLACE NEW DELHI")
  - banner DcapWnEpQSG ("130 HOG MARKET, RAJINDRA PLACE, NEW DELHI")
  - YT community post ("130 HOG MARKET RAJINDRA PLACE NEW DELHI 110008")
  - captions ("Rajendra Place")
- Hog Market is real and sits at Rajendra Place. Maps listings there include Shimla 251 Hog Market, S-175 and S-200, all "Rajendra Place 110008".
- "Rajendra Market" is a different place: Tis Hazari, Delhi 110006 (28.66409, 77.21842), about 4.6 km northeast. India Post's 110054 is Civil Lines / Tis Hazari; 110008 is Patel Nagar, which includes Rajendra Place.
- WhatsApp's "130, Rajendra Market, New Delhi 110054" is almost certainly a mix-up. The client should fix it and spell "Rajendra" consistently (IG and the banner say "Rajindra").
- **Map pin:** the Hog Market point 28.6446717, 77.1772307 is shared with the Shimla 251 listing, so it's a general market point, not the exact shop. TODO: exact pin.
